# Card design: original ratios and colour vibes

This is the reference for anyone making Turtle Party cards, and for the server when it builds a bundle.

**The rule:** new cards change the effect while keeping that card's theme. They don't change its colour, its value or the deck's ratios. The Alpha deck's ratios have been playtested, so they're the baseline.

## Colour vibes

The colours and their feel, in Tommy's words (2026-11-09):

| Colour | Element | Vibe | Typical effects |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Red | Fire | Chaotic | Discarding, burning cards away |
| Orange | Wind | Swift | Fast moves that shift the game state |
| Blue | Water | Wizardry | Tricky, magical plays |
| Green | Earth | Strong | Sturdy, powerful cards |
| Yellow | — | Party favors | Ongoing Effects |
| Purple | — | Wild | Every colour at once, pairs with anything |

The "typical effects" column is a short reading of the vibe. When the Alpha deck is counted (below), it gets checked against the real cards, and any surprises go here.

## Original ratios (Alpha deck, 120 cards)

**Not counted yet.** This is waiting on the Alpha PDF, which Tommy is adding to the repo. Once it's in, this section will give:

What Tommy has already said about the balance:

- **Discard Anytime cards are almost all 1s.** That makes them fairly disposable. They're handy for finishing a same-colour pair, at the cost of giving up their protective or defensive use.
- The colours, numbers and types have many similar balances. The count will make them visible.
